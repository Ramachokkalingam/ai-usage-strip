# Code of Conduct

AI Usage Strip is a small open source project, and we want everyone who uses or works on it to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain the reasoning behind it.
- Accept that people disagree, and that the maintainer makes the final call on what gets merged.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, [@Ramachokkalingam](https://github.com/Ramachokkalingam), through GitHub. If the matter is sensitive, use the repository's private reporting form (**Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and say in the title that it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer will review every report and may respond by talking it through, giving a warning, removing comments or code, or blocking someone from the project, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
